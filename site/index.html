How to install

Open the DMG, drag Babu App Uninstaller to Applications, then eject the disk image. This build is unsigned and not notarized.

macOS 15 and later
Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

macOS 14 and earlier
In Finder, right-click the app and choose Open.

Terminal
xattr -cr "/Applications/Babu App Uninstaller.app"
